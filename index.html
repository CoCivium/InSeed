The work

Begin with problems people recognize.

These are potential engagement areas, not representations of completed customer engagements or commercially released software.

01 / Diagnose

Governance and process review

Understand confusing roles, slow decisions, dispute handling, accountability gaps and the costs of keeping people informed.

Output to test: a bounded problem map and a reviewable baseline.
02 / Prototype

Small, reversible improvements

Explore opt-in workflow helpers, accessible interfaces and decision support that respect existing systems and affected people's rights.

Output to test: one useful task with a stop and recovery path.
03 / Verify

Evidence before expansion

Challenge the proposed benefit, document limitations and measure actual results before describing an experiment as operational success.

Output to test: a readable evidence and limitations record.
Working principles

One accountable step at a time.

Automation and AI can help, but they do not replace authorization, privacy, human rights or independent validation.

  1. STEP 01

    Define

    Agree on the problem, intended users, actual authority and what must remain private.

  2. STEP 02

    Compare

    Check the current baseline and serious alternatives, including doing nothing.

  3. STEP 03

    Try

    Run a small, reversible and consent-aware pilot with clear success and stop criteria.

  4. STEP 04

    Verify

    Preserve results, contradictions, costs and the path to correction or independent recovery.
